Moss walls

Long-lasting and low maintenance, our spectacular moss walls are endlessly flexible to complement and enhance any style of interior workplace design.

The benefits of moss walls

For workers

Moss walls provide a beautiful indoor connection to nature that lifts mood and reduces stress, making a workspace a pleasure to be in.

For business

As well as creating spaces that attract staff, moss walls can be shaped into logos or images that reinforce brand image in a fresh new way.

Easy care

Our sustainably grown and harvested moss needs no water, light or maintenance to stay looking great for years to come.

Planting the ideas to transform your workplace

Entrance areas

The versatility of moss can be used to create stunning reception walls that wow visitors, even incorporating brand logos and signage.

Desk & workspace

Moss walls bring attractive colour and texture to inspire and uplift those working alongside, without impinging on the available floor space.

Corridors

Where there is not enough natural light to support real plants, moss walls can be used instead to bring in natural beauty.

Kitchens

Adding a moss wall to a kitchen will soften sounds and create a relaxed, welcoming ambience where people can de-stress.

Meeting spaces

Meeting spaces should be designed to foster creativity and collaboration. Installing a colourful moss wall does exactly that.

Breakout rooms

A moss wall creates a perfect backdrop for large spaces where people relax or gather for company events.

From drawing board to finished scheme

We offer a complete package, from initial design support, through on-site installation to ongoing maintenance to ensure it looks as great in five years as it did on the first day.

FAQs

Do living moss walls require maintenance?
Our moss walls are naturally preserved so require zero maintenance (feather dusting bi-annually).
What is a ‘Class 1 fire-rated’ moss wall? 
Our fire-rated moss walls are certified to Class B-s1-d0 under EN 13501-1:2019, a leading European fire safety standard. This rating means the panels have very limited flame spread, minimal smoke production, and produce no flaming droplets, making them suitable for use in high-footfall commercial spaces.
Where can fire-rated moss walls be used?
Vantage Spaces moss walls are ideal for corridors, lobbies, receptions, stairwells, and other areas that require enhanced fire safety compliance. Because they require virtually no-maintenance, our moss walls are perfect for offices, hospitality spaces, and public buildings that need to meet strict fire safety regulations.
Do you operate nationwide?
Yes, however we predominantly operate in London (and surrounding areas), Manchester, Leeds and Newcastle, but we do deliver projects nationwide depending on the project size and location.
Are the fire-rated moss panels still natural?
 Yes. Our fire-rated panels use the same high-quality preserved moss, treated with non-toxic, fire-resistant agents to ensure safety without compromising on aesthetic appeal, texture, or sustainability. Vantage Spaces moss walls retain the natural look and feel of standard moss walls, with the added benefit of enhanced fire performance.

Class 1 fire-rated moss walls for enhanced safety

Transform your workspace with the natural beauty of moss – now available in a Class 1 fire-rated option. Our fire-rated moss walls meet stringent UK and European fire safety standards (EN 13501-1:2019, Class B-s1-d0), making them ideal for commercial interiors, including corridors, lobbies, and high-footfall areas.

Designed for modern workspaces, these panels offer all the biophilic and acoustic benefits of preserved moss, with added fire safety performance and compliance peace of mind.
