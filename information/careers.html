The Vantage Process

Our heritage

Vantage Spaces is a family business based in Leeds. We've been trading since 1977, and have collaborated on providing living and replica plants, green walls and large trees for FTSE 250 businesses and incredible buildings such as ‘London’s first vertical village’ – 22 Bishopsgate.

Planting the places where great teams thrive.

Following the pandemic, businesses have placed an increasing emphasis on creating spaces that employees want to return to, encouraging collaboration, creativity and improved wellbeing. We believe that biophilia – our innate connection to nature – is vital to achieving this. Creating these experiences for staff within workplace settings has led to the Vantage Spaces team working closely with designers and end clients, to integrate greenery in all areas of interior environments.
