We're planting paradises time after time

Since the first day we put our own roots down, we’ve believed in the power that interior landscaping has to create special spaces in which people spend time. Working practices may change, but the value of a hub where people can interact, integrate and collaborate never changes. That's why we're still passionate about creating spaces where people give their best.
Vantage Spaces. Bringing space alive.

The Vantage journey

SCROLL TO DISCOVER →
1977

Prospect Plant Display established by Martin and Jackie Edwards from their home in Duckworth Lane, Bradford.

1979

Current MD of Vantage, Robin Edwards, joins his parents in the business to support its development.

1985

Premises purchased at 254 Upper Woodlands Road, Bradford.

1989

Business relocated to Aston Mount, Bramley, Leeds.
‍

2001

Expanded office space came into use across the road in Bramley.

2013

Vantage Spaces relocated to Vitality House, Milford Place, Leeds.
