EnviroZone: flexible workspace biophilia

In today’s evolving workplace, adaptability is everything. Teams need spaces that flex to different tasks – from focused individual work to collaborative group sessions – without losing the comfort and wellbeing benefits that biophilic design brings. And that’s where EnviroZone comes in.

Blog

Contents

EnviroZone – our mobile planter system – combines beautiful planting with functional space zoning, giving workspaces a natural, breathable way to define areas without the hard edges of partitions. It’s a smart choice for businesses that want to make their office spaces greener, healthier, and more versatile.

Why EnviroZone works

EnviroZone planters are designed to integrate planting directly into the fabric of the workspace while remaining fully flexible. Key features include:

+ Mobile and adaptable – Lockable castors mean planters can be moved and reconfigured as teams or layouts change.
‍

+ Natural zoning – Wooden slats and lush planting define spaces without cutting off light or visibility.
‍

+ Easy to maintain – Integrated impermeable liners make watering and plant care simple.
‍

+ Customisable – Available in multiple levels, with optional whiteboard configuration.
‍

+ Sustainable – Compatible with our peat-free EnviroLeaf planting system in living, replica, or preserved formats.
‍

+ Style to suit every space – Choose from a wide palette of finishes, including Light Oak, Dark Oak, and bold RAL colours.
‍

Case Study: NG Bailey, Leeds

When fellow Leeds-born company NG Bailey underwent office refurbishment, they were on the lookout for planting that would complement their new furniture-led fit out. Working with Wagstaff, we integrated greenery seamlessly into the scheme to give it the true Vantage Spaces touch.

We made use of existing planters provided by the client, planting them up with bold, bushy displays that formed natural hubs in collaboration and teapoint areas. For the high tables, we introduced stem variation using small trees with twisted branches and variegated foliage, adding depth and visual interest.

The result was a vibrant, biophilic workspace that reflects both NG Bailey’s innovation and its Leeds heritage.

EnviroZone configurations

Every workplace is different, which is why EnviroZone is available in multiple formats:

‍

+ Single or double-level units – Ideal for creating different degrees of privacy and separation.

‍

+ Whiteboard configurations – Perfect for agile teams that need collaboration and idea-sharing tools.

‍

+ Custom dimensions – While standard units measure 1200 x 1500 x 400mm, EnviroZone can be made completely bespoke.

‍

+ Colour finishes – From subtle neutrals to statement brights, over 20 finishes are available to complement your workspace design.

Bringing nature into flexible workspaces

EnviroZone delivers more than just greenery. It provides a mobile, practical way to shape the workspace around people’s needs while keeping wellbeing and sustainability at the core. Whether it’s breaking up large floor plates, softening acoustics, or giving teams more ownership of their environment, EnviroZone is an amazing tool for bringing bold biophilia to your space.

How to buy:

There are different ways to pay for your Vantage planting scheme, depending on what elements it contains.

Purchase

Elements that are constructed specifically for your office and could not be re-used elsewhere must be purchased outright, but other units and plants can be purchased outright too. In this case, we recommend our aftercare service to keep your scheme looking as good as it does when it’s first installed, but the choice is yours.

Rental

This is often a great way to pay for your planting scheme. We supply your plants and their display units and containers in return for a monthly payment by direct debit. Our rental contracts usually run for an initial three years, and then continue for as long as you want. Your monthly payment covers not just rental costs but also our full aftercare service. A hassle-free, cash-flow friendly option.

Tailored to you

Whether outright purchase or rental, with or without aftercare – or a mix of these – we can take you through the different options available and create a package that’s right for you.
