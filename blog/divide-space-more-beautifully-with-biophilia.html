Divide space more beautifully with biophilia!

Although open plan spaces are common within office environments, interior designers and architects are generally careful to create different areas within these spaces so that they don’t feel too cavernous or exposed. Planting systems can be a very effective way of dividing up space without using ‘hard’ solid surfaces such as walls or dividers, which can sometimes feel a bit sterile and too ‘enclosed’.

Biophilic Office Dividers: Creative Ways to Zone Your Workspace with Plants

Open-plan offices have become the norm, but without thoughtful design, these large, open spaces can feel exposed, noisy, and impersonal. While traditional dividers or walls can solve privacy issues, they often create a sterile, closed-off atmosphere. That’s where biophilic office dividers come in. By using plants to define zones, you can create a workspace that feels calm, connected, and inspiring, while still allowing light and air to flow freely. From modular green screens to mobile planters, plant-based partitions are both functional and beautiful.
‍

The Benefits of Plant-Based Office Dividers

Planting systems can transform how a workspace looks, feels, and functions:

  • Boost creativity and productivity by bringing nature indoors
  • Reduce noise pollution in busy open-plan environments
  • Add privacy without creating barriers
  • Maximise floor space by combining planting with partitions
  • Break up the ‘sea of desks’ for a more inviting layout
  • Reconfigure layouts easily with mobile or modular designs

‍

ParaVert & Hevea – Flexible Modular Green Screens

These modular plant-holder systems can be configured straight, curved, or wavy, at any height, to form striking biophilic screens. They’re perfect for:

  • Partitioning workstations without blocking collaboration
  • Adding cosy zones to breakout or kitchen areas
  • Creating softer boundaries in high-traffic spaces

Because they’re open and airy, ParaVert & Hevea allow views through while still adding privacy and greenery.

‍

EnviroZone – Mobile Planters for Flexible Zoning

Available in single or double height, EnviroZone planters are designed for maximum flexibility. Move them like furniture to:

  • Break up large reception or event areas
  • Instantly create private collaboration spaces
  • Add concentrated bursts of greenery to underused corners

They can be filled with live or replica plants, making them perfect for low-light areas too.

‍

Palisade Greenery – Slim Shelving with Living Plants

Ideal for space-conscious offices, palisade shelving combines planter boxes or moss panels with open shelves, creating a stylish divider that doesn’t block light. Choose from:

  • Chequered or random planting patterns
  • Bespoke designs in brand colours
  • Options that display books, awards, or decorative items alongside plants

‍

Joinery Planting – Custom-Built Green Partitions

For a tailored, brand-specific solution, joinery planting can be designed to fit your exact space and style. Popular in wayfinding layouts, these dividers guide movement through an office while providing soft visual barriers. Repeating a single plant variety can create a bold, unified look.

Floor-Standing Troughs & Planters – Simple, Effective Screening

A budget-friendly option, these planters work well in reception and seating areas to soften large, open spaces. Available in a range of finishes, they can be moved or re-planted seasonally for variety.

‍

‍

2025 Trends in Biophilic Office Dividers

In 2025, plant-based partitions are moving beyond aesthetics, they’re central to workspace wellbeing and sustainability. Designers are opting for:

  • Modular systems that adapt to hybrid work patterns
  • Plant combinations that purify air and boost mood
  • Mixed live and replica planting for low-maintenance greenery

How to buy:

There are different ways to pay for your Vantage planting scheme, depending on what elements it contains.

Purchase

Elements that are constructed specifically for your office and could not be re-used elsewhere must be purchased outright, but other units and plants can be purchased outright too. In this case, we recommend our aftercare service to keep your scheme looking as good as it does when it’s first installed, but the choice is yours.

Rental

This is often a great way to pay for your planting scheme. We supply your plants and their display units and containers in return for a monthly payment by direct debit. Our rental contracts usually run for an initial three years, and then continue for as long as you want. Your monthly payment covers not just rental costs but also our full aftercare service. A hassle-free, cash-flow friendly option.
