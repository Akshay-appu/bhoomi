FAQs

Do living moss walls require maintenance?
Our moss walls are naturally preserved so require zero maintenance (feather dusting bi-annually).
Do you do external walls?
No, we don't do external facades or external walls, only internal.
Do you do small jobs or domestic work?
Our projects usually start from £1,000 and no we don't do domestic work.
Do you operate nationwide?
Yes, however we predominantly operate in London (and surrounding areas), Manchester, Leeds and Newcastle, but we do deliver projects nationwide depending on the project size and location.
What do EnviroWall greenwalls need to be fixed to?
Moss or replica panels can be screwed directly into an existing wall but we recommend our clients fit 12mm plyboard to the wall prior to installation.
