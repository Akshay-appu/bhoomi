Plant advice

Bonsai: a tree, but not a tree!

We often think of bonsai as tiny trees, but technically, bonsai is the art or practice of cultivating these miniature wonders – not the trees themselves! The practice of bonsai involves growing miniature versions of tree species in small pots, using cultivation techniques such as pruning, root reduction, potting and defoliation. So, a bonsai tree is not a single species, but a tree of any species that has been grown using bonsai methods. Out of all the trees we install at Vantage, bonsai trees attract the most awe and wonder. They are captivating and mystical, often imparting a deep sense of tranquillity – no wonder they are regarded as natural works of art all over the world!
