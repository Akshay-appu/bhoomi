FAQs

Do your Replica Walls require any maintenance?
No maintenance isn’t needed that’s the beauty of Replica Planting! We do recommend a light feather dusting every 6 months.
Do your Preserved Moss Walls require any maintenance?
No maintenance is needed for this product! As the moss has been harvested from the forest floor then preserved in a saline solution and natural food dye it’s no longer growing.
Do you offer Bespoke Green Walls?
Yes! Replica & Preserved walls can be bespoke and to any design! Please send over your intended visual / look to our design team and we’ll bring your vision to life.
